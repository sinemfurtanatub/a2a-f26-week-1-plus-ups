# Workspace builder

Plus-up for the A2A Fall 2026 Week 1 presentation, slides 44 "The 3 parts of a project" and 45 "Pick where your files live".

- `embed.html`: 708x400 (16:9) stage for a Notion embed. Never scrolls.
- `index.html`: the same tool on a full page.

Part 1, "The four files": click File 1 to File 4 (About Your Company, Ideal Customer Profile, Products and Offerings, Testimonials and Proof) to see what goes in each. Wording is from the old slide image.
Part 2, "Pick where your files live": pick Google Drive, Notion, Desktop folders or Dropbox. Each shows the example folder tree from slide 45. Setup steps for each location are not on the page yet and show "Coming soon" until the page has them.

No analytics, accounts or network calls. Fonts and logo are embedded. Season Mix is a trial font: confirm the license before publishing publicly.

Status: local build only, not published.
