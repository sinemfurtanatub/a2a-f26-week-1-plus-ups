# Your Business Is a Kitchen

Plus-up for the A2A Fall 2026 Week 1 presentation, slide 11 area ("Your Business Is a Kitchen"). It replaces the static image.

- `embed.html`: 708x400 (16:9) stage for a Notion embed. Never scrolls.
- `index.html`: the same tool on a full page.

Tabs: Marketing, Sales (by department), Service Biz, Real Estate, Coaching (by industry) and "Your business". Each tab lists its recipe examples from the old image. "Your business" asks "What dishes do you make from scratch every single week?". Arrow keys move between tabs.

All wording comes from the old slide image. No analytics, accounts or network calls. Fonts and logo are embedded. Season Mix is a trial font: confirm the license before publishing publicly.

Status: local build only, not published.
